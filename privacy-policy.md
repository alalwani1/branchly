# Privacy Policy & Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Branchly** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with our application and services, including integrations via the Meta Platforms and Instagram APIs.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate or use our application with your Meta or Instagram account, we may collect:
* **Account Information:** Basic public profile information (such as Instagram username, User ID, and profile picture URL).
* **Platform Data:** Authorized Instagram data granted via permissions (e.g., account insights, media metadata, or message interactions), strictly as required to provide our service.
* **Technical Information:** Log data and request metadata necessary for system performance and security.

We do **not** collect, store, or process your account passwords or private payment details.

---

## 2. How We Use the Information

We process your data strictly to:
* Provide, maintain, and operate the core features of the application.
* Authenticate your identity and verify authorization via Instagram Login.
* Troubleshoot technical issues and enhance platform stability.

We do **not** sell, rent, or lease your personal data or Platform Data to third parties. We do not use Platform Data for surveillance or unauthorized profiling.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the application's functionality or until you revoke access or request deletion. When access is revoked or upon account termination, associated platform tokens and identifiers are permanently purged from our systems.

---

## 4. User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies, you have full control over your data. If you wish to delete your data associated with Branchly, you can do so at any time:

### Method 1: Remove App Access via Instagram / Facebook
1. Go to your Instagram profile **Settings and activity** -> **Website permissions** -> **Apps and websites**.
2. Find **Branchly** in the active list.
3. Tap **Remove**. Once removed, our app will no longer receive updates or access to your profile.

### Method 2: Request Full Data Deletion via Email
To request complete deletion of all records and data associated with your account from our servers:
* Send an email to: `contact@amitlalwani.com` *(or your preferred developer contact email)*
* **Subject:** `Data Deletion Request - Branchly`
* Include your Instagram handle / User ID.
* We will verify the request and delete all stored tokens and records within **30 business days**, confirming via return email once completed.
